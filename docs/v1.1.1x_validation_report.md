## Before/after comparison

First v1.1.0x pass on the same fixed benchmark:

- matched injected signals: 11 / 15
- unmatched injected signals: 4
- unmatched final candidates: 21
- mean absolute duration error among matched signals: 2.219 s
- mean absolute recovered-SNR error among matched signals: 1.732 dB

Final v1.1.1x pass:

- matched injected signals: 13 / 15
- unmatched injected signals: 2
- unmatched final candidates: 0
- mean absolute duration error among matched signals: 1.440 s
- median absolute duration error among matched signals: 0.999 s
- mean absolute recovered-SNR error among matched signals: 0.824 dB
- median absolute recovered-SNR error among matched signals: 0.513 dB

The two unmatched injected signals are short 4-second tracks at the difficult end of this benchmark: `sig_snr2p0_4s` and `sig_snr4p0_4s`. The 2 dB 4-second miss is expected at this sensitivity. The 4 dB 4-second miss appears as a very short singleton event below the final candidate gate; promoting all such singletons would reintroduce the earlier false-candidate problem, so I did not weaken the default gate to force this one case.

## Assessment of candidate morphology

The final review produced 13 candidates and no unmatched final candidates. The generated review contact sheet shows weak but track-consistent narrowband events. I did not find radial/clipping morphology or the earlier repeated single-tile false-candidate pattern in the final candidate list.

## Remaining limitations

At the edge of detectability, short low-SNR tracks can still be missed or partially recovered. That is an expected limitation of a conservative continuous-narrowband candidate gate. The fix in v1.1.1x deliberately favors reliability over forcing every 4-second near-threshold event into the candidate list.
